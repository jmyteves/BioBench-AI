Scoring rubric

Four dimensions of scientific reasoning

Not whether the model is correct — whether it thinks like a scientist facing genuine uncertainty.

01
Novelty
Does the model generate non-obvious hypotheses beyond textbook retrieval? Would a domain expert find the response interesting?
02
Tractability
Are proposed experiments technically feasible and well-specified? Could a postdoc follow the design without major revision?
03
Mechanistic Depth
Does the model trace complete molecular causal chains, or describe outcomes without explaining how? No hand-waving permitted.
04
Confounder Awareness
Does the model identify alternative explanations and methodological limits? Would a rigorous peer reviewer be satisfied?
Prompt design

Six categories, six failure modes

Each category is designed to expose a specific way AI models fail at scientific reasoning.

HG · 5 prompts
Hypothesis Generation
Given unexpected data, can the model generate mechanistically distinct, non-obvious hypotheses — not just list known possibilities?
Primary: Novelty
ED · 5 prompts
Experimental Design
Can the model design a real, executable experiment with appropriate controls that actually answers the question asked?
Primary: Tractability · Confounder Awareness
ISD · 5 prompts
Interpret Surprising Data
When a result contradicts established knowledge, does the model reason mechanistically or retreat to description and deflection?
Primary: Mechanistic Depth · Confounder Awareness
CDS · 5 prompts
Cross-Domain Synthesis
Can the model connect mechanisms across metabolism, biophysics, and chromatin biology — and make a specific, testable prediction?
Primary: Novelty · Mechanistic Depth
CBS · 5 prompts
Critique & Blind Spots
Given a study's conclusions, can the model identify methodological limits, confounders, and alternative interpretations a reviewer would raise?
Primary: Confounder Awareness
MD · 5 prompts
Mechanistic Depth
Can the model trace a complete molecular chain — naming the players, directionality, and interactions — without hand-waving any steps?
Primary: Mechanistic Depth
Sample prompts

Selected from the benchmark

Six prompts — one from each category. The full set is in the repository.

#01Hypothesis Generation
A transcription factor knockout in mouse ESCs causes unexpected upregulation of 200 previously silent genes. What are the three most mechanistically distinct hypotheses that could explain this result?
#06Experimental Design
You want to test whether H3K27me3 loss is necessary and sufficient for cell fate reprogramming — not merely correlated with it. Design a rigorous experiment that distinguishes causality from correlation.
#11Interpret Surprising Data
ATAC-seq reveals that chromatin accessibility at a set of enhancers increases during differentiation, but the putative target genes remain transcriptionally silenced. What are the most mechanistically distinct explanations for this uncoupling?
#18Cross-Domain Synthesis
Mechanical forces on cells alter both cytoskeletal organisation and gene expression. Propose a complete mechanistic chain from the initial force-sensing event at the membrane to a durable change in transcriptional output in the nucleus.
#25Critique & Blind Spots
A ChIP-seq study identifies 5,000 genomic binding sites for a transcription factor and claims all sites are functional regulatory elements based on peak calling with a standard FDR threshold. What are the critical caveats?
#29Mechanistic Depth
Explain how a point mutation in a CTCF binding site within a topologically associating domain boundary could lead to ectopic gene activation in a tissue where that gene is normally permanently silenced.
How to use it

Run it against any model

Interactive browser tool, Python CLI runner, or raw JSON — all in the repository.

  • 01Clone the repo and load prompts.json
  • 02Run prompts against your target model
  • 03Score each response 1–4 on each dimension
  • 04Export results as JSON or CSV
